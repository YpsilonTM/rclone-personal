Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

rclone-personal is a personal Google OAuth client used with rclone to access the operator’s own cloud storage (for example Google Drive) for private backup and sync.

Who this applies to

This app is operated solely by Yuri Hackl (yurihackl@gmail.com) for personal use. It is not offered to the public and has no end users other than the operator.

Data we access

When you authorize the app, Google may grant access to:

  • Your Google account identity (email) for the consent screen
  • Cloud storage files and folders that rclone creates or syncs

File content stays in your Google account. This project does not run a separate cloud service that stores, sells, or shares your files.

How data is used

Access is used only to upload, update, list, and delete files via rclone on machines you control. Tokens are stored locally in your rclone configuration.

Sharing

We do not sell or share Google account data with third parties. Google’s own processing is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention

OAuth tokens remain until you revoke access in your Google Account permissions or delete the local rclone remote. Synced files remain until you delete them.
